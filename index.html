Dots & Dashes

10×10 · Medium Left 0 0:00

Solved

How to play
  1. Fill every cell with a dot or a dash . Tap a cell to cycle: empty, dot, dash.
  2. Never more than two of the same mark next to each other, across or down. is not allowed.
  3. Every row and every column has the same number of dots and dashes. On a 10×10 grid that is five of each.
  4. No two rows are identical, and no two columns are identical.

Shaded cells are given and can't be changed. Each puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone.
On a keyboard: arrows to move, 1 or . for a dot, 2 or - for a dash, Backspace to clear, Ctrl+Z to undo. Right-click cycles backwards.
